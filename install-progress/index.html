WORKSPACE HELP

See the schedule on the model, on any date.

Start here

  1. Home: 3D, the floor sheet and the P6 Gantt open together, on the project's first start date.
  2. Timeline: drag the slider, or click or drag the Gantt's date scale, to see the model on that day. Play runs the schedule from the shown date.
  3. Colors: walls take the color of the latest drywall or framing activity started by the shown date. Walls not reached yet, and other trades' work, stay ghosted.
  4. Stages: the stages come from the schedule itself (its stage code, or the activity name without the floor). Untick a stage to hide it; click its swatch to change its color.
  5. Focus: click an activity to show only its walls, with its quantities. Search keywords narrow the activities that color the model.
  6. Floors apart: every floor at once with the schedule's share per floor; Gap sets the space between floors. All plans shows every floor sheet.
  7. Select a wall in 3D or the sheet to see its properties and related P6 activities.

Your files and progress

Connect data
Preview Revit, schedule, cost and Internal Production Tracking Tool mapping with sample records. Labour costs are included in the project total. Demo links stay in this browser; no external system sync is enabled.
Model review
The three buttons inside 3D offer isometric, top and fit without changing the floor. Model tools keeps the screenshot action.
Import a schedule
Use Upload in Schedule, or Connect in the Schedule step. PDF, XER, XLSX and CSV are supported. Your file is read in this browser.
Play schedule
Play moves the date one working day at a time; walls take their stage color as their activity starts. Pause holds the date. Colors show the schedule, not verified field completion.
Export your work
Open the schedule's ⋯ menu and choose Export CSV for the activity comparison.
Save and reset
Edits save automatically in this browser. Start over with demo data restores the demonstration schedule after confirmation. Other browsers have their own copy; changes are not shared with a team.
What the colors mean
Each schedule stage has its own color (change it in the Stages legend). Pale: the picked activity before it starts. Ghosted: not reached yet, or other trades. The model geometry stays read-only.
Connection help
If the model cannot connect, check your internet connection and Retry. This site needs Autodesk services; PDF import also needs jsDelivr.
Presentation
Present opens full screen on desktop. This is a sample workspace: the schedule is synthetic, not live project reporting.
