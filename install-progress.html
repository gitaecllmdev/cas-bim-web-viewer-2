WORKSPACE HELP

Track the work. See what comes next.

Start here

  1. Home: 3D, the floor plan and Gantt schedule open together. Selecting a wall follows its floor, keeps the whole floor in view, and highlights the same wall in both views.
  2. Overview: review the priority activities and illustrative cost forecast. Click an item to see its model context.
  3. Resize: drag the divider between model and dashboard, or use Model / Both / Schedule. Arrow keys also move the divider.
  4. Choose a level above the model to focus on one floor. Choose All levels to return to the building.
  5. Progress by floor: change a level's install stage, or select walls in the model and update only those walls. Undo restores the previous change.
  6. Timeline: click an activity to see its walls, dates and progress. Use the filters to find open work.
  7. Calendar: click a day number to preview planned installation. Click an activity bar for its details.
  8. Next 3 weeks: review three weeks of open work. Change the start date or click a task for model context.

Your files and progress

Connect data
Preview Revit, schedule, cost and Internal Production Tracking Tool mapping with sample records. Labour costs are included in the project total. Demo links stay in this browser; no external system sync is enabled.
Model review
The three buttons inside 3D offer isometric, top and fit without changing the floor. The adjacent status selector switches Installed, Vs plan and Schedule colors. Model tools keeps the screenshot action.
Import a schedule
Use Upload in Schedule, or Connect in the Schedule step. PDF, XER, XLSX and CSV are supported. Your file is read in this browser.
Play schedule
Play reveals walls at activity starts and updates their stage colors. It starts with All levels or the selected floor. Pause holds the date; Play resumes. Choose Installed to restore the normal model. Playback colors show the scheduled stage, not verified field completion.
Export your work
Open the schedule's ⋯ menu and choose Export CSV for the activity comparison.
Save and reset
Edits save automatically in this browser. Start over with demo data restores the demonstration schedule and field stages after confirmation. Other browsers have their own copy; changes are not shared with a team.
What the colors mean
Amber: in progress. Green: finished or on plan. Red: behind plan or at risk. The model geometry stays read-only.
Connection help
If the model cannot connect, check your internet connection and Retry. This site needs Autodesk services; PDF import also needs jsDelivr.
Presentation
Present opens full screen on desktop. This is a sample workspace: schedule and field progress are synthetic, not live project reporting.
