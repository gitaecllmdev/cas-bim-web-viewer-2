Panel shops

Every framing shop drawing: cut list, member tags, ordinates to studs, tracks and openings. Search by panel, level or room; one click opens the sheet (PDF download and print there).

Takeoff

The 3D model and plan on top, quantities underneath: pick a stud, track or board line, or a member mark, to see its walls. Studs, track, headers, sills and cripples by mark and cut length, board and finish; CSV.

Open the takeoff › ▤

Schedule

The P6 schedule in the install progress tracker: a Gantt chart and a calendar under the model. Pick an activity to see its walls; slide the date to see the model as planned that day, or installed vs plan.

Open the schedule ›

Tools no model required

⇄

P6 Converter

A standalone tool: upload a P6 schedule PDF, find activities with several keywords, check the ones you want, edit and download Excel or CSV. Processed privately in your browser.

Open P6 Converter › ⌂

Panel Shop Generator

Framing shop drawings without a model: type a panel's length and height or draw its shape (single slope, gable, any top), add doors and windows, pick or type the studs, track, headers, jambs and sills. Cut list, tags and dimensions as you type; PDF for one panel or a whole set; JSON to keep or share.

Open the generator › ▦

Ceiling Generator

Lay out hardlid framing or ceiling grid. Edit the footprint, grid origin, openings and fixtures; review plans, quantities and a drawing. Save projects, download PDF or export the layout.

Open ceiling generator › ▤

Batch Sheets

A whole level of walls exported from Revit: see them in 3D, pick the walls to frame, apply framing settings to them together and download one sheet per panel, the framing config and labels. Files stay in your browser.

Open Batch Sheets › ▣

Label Maker

Panel and member labels with QR codes and your company logo. Import a spreadsheet, paste a cut list or extract an image, review quantities and print 2 × 4-inch label sheets. Files stay in your browser.

Open Label Maker › ≡

Criteria Review

Open an engineering criteria workbook and review what it says: each style's source name broken into its fields, the columns' values with their units and states, the dropdown label, and where a name and its columns disagree. Read privately in your browser; nothing is kept.

Open Criteria Review ›
